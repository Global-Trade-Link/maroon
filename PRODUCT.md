# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (user's choice). Demo phase: no backend, no checkout; state lives in the browser.

## Users

Shoppers buying gold-tone jewelry for themselves or as gifts, mostly on phones, who want a piece that feels personal. Their job: pick a bracelet or necklace and dress it with charms that mean something to them, see it before buying, know the price as they go.

## Product Purpose

An online shop for a jewelry seller whose core products are bracelets and necklaces. The differentiator is an interactive customizer: choose a base, add charms, see the piece update live, get a running price. Success: a shopper finishes a custom piece and adds it to cart.

## Positioning

Not a catalog of fixed SKUs. Every piece is composable: base + charms, arranged by the shopper, priced transparently. The customizer is the product.

## Operating Context

Landing page (maximalist, shows the product range) and a customizer surface. Real product photography will be supplied by the user later; the demo uses illustrated/CSS-rendered stand-ins that must be easy to swap.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: bracelets, necklaces; charms attach to either.
- Customizer (confirmed scope): pick base type (bracelet/necklace), pick chain/material, add/remove/reorder charms on a live preview, running price, add-to-cart summary.
- Not in demo scope (undecided): engraving, sizing, gift packaging, payments, accounts.
- Brand: Maroon (from the supplied logo: cream ground, maroon wordmark, wreath monogram).
- Vibe (user's binding constraint): gold / luxe / ornate; visual direction is maximalist.

## Brand Commitments

- Maximalist landing page (user's explicit requirement).
- Gold / luxe / ornate register (user's choice).
- Wordmark and monogram supplied by the user (assets/logo.jpg).

## Evidence on Hand

Real product photography supplied for three charms (red mushroom, evil palm, dance shoe) and three chains (2.0 mm fine cable, 4.14 mm rolo, 9.9 mm paperclip). No testimonials, press, or pricing supplied. Demo prices and product names are illustrative placeholders and must not be presented as real. Remaining charm photography arrives later from the user.

## Product Principles

1. The customizer is the hero: every path on the site leads to it.
2. Price is always visible while composing; no surprises at checkout.
3. Abundance over restraint: show range, layering, and charm variety generously.
4. Swap-ready: every visual stand-in must be replaceable by a real photo without layout changes.
