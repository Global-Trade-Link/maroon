# Storefront checkout

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing storefront identity, as requested: shared Masthead and Footer, cream surfaces, maroon script headings, serif supporting copy, black sans product details and labelled controls. No separate customer account or order-status surface.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Checkout feels like the same boutique, while making delivery, payment and confirmation easy to complete.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Maroon tokens, existing script/serif/sans voices, pill CTAs, product photography and live custom-piece previews. Warm tinted summary, hairline form sections, generous space.

STORY: Review and edit custom pieces in the bag; enter required email and delivery details; submit manual payment details; land on a thank-you page. Email carries subsequent confirmation and delivery updates. No customer status navigation or sample notices.

Recovery: a persistent reminder and header Payment link return to the saved unpaid order after navigation/reload. Bag and checkout provide a resume action, including an empty bag. New bag items remain separate; show the saved order's payment amount explicitly. Clear recovery on submission, payment confirmation, cancellation or definitive missing-order response, while retaining it during network errors. Repeated ready-made adds update quantity in one row; custom designs keep independent editable rows.

Payment validation: require either a transaction reference of at least three characters or a screenshot. Put actionable errors beneath the relevant field, associate them with the input, focus/scroll the first invalid field into view on submission, and clear corrected errors as the customer edits. Show server failures next to the submit action and move focus there.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared storefront nav; centered bag/delivery/payment progress, script title and short lede; product list or labelled form left and order summary right. Mobile stacks summary after content. Confirmation centers acknowledgement above summary/address.

FORM: User-specified extension of the established storefront. seed: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
